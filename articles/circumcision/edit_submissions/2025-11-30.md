# Circumcision: draft correction request (2025-11-30)

**Status:** an early draft correction request for the Grokipedia Circumcision article, written against the [2025-11-30 snapshot](../snapshots/2025-11-30.txt). It states the author's view and was not produced by this repo's scan method. Its citations have not been re-checked in this repo, and the repo does not record whether or how it was submitted.

**Author's view:** the article leans toward non-therapeutic infant male circumcision (NTIMC). It presents contested preventive benefits as decisive and gives little space to what is removed and to the consent question.

## Problems raised (author's view, with the sources the author cited)

1. **The anatomy and function of the foreskin get little coverage**  
   Taylor 1996, Cold & Taylor 1999 and Sorrells 2007 describe the foreskin as densely innervated tissue and report fine-touch sensitivity findings. The removal is permanent. The article treats this as incidental.

2. **Benefits are marginal or context-specific in developed nations**  
   - Infant UTI: baseline risk ~1 %; NNT ≈ 100–111 (AAP 2012 expired policy)  
   - Penile cancer: lifetime risk <1/100 000 in developed nations (CDC 2023)  
   - HIV: 60 % relative reduction from African adult voluntary trials; not applicable to neonatal circumcision in low-prevalence heterosexual settings or MSM (Templeton 2009, Wiysonge 2011)

3. **Proxy consent is treated as settled**  
   The article frames parental proxy consent as ethically valid for non-therapeutic genital surgery. Bodies and rulings that take the opposite view include:  
   - German Regional Court Cologne 2012 (criminal bodily harm)  
   - Royal Dutch Medical Association 2010 (postpone until age of consent)  
   - Council of Europe Resolution 1952 (2013)  
   - Brussels Collaboration on Bodily Integrity (2019) – 40+ scholars: “medically unnecessary genital cutting on non-consenting children violates bodily integrity”

4. **Complication rates are selectively quoted**  
   The article uses the lowest figures (0.2–0.5 %) from US hospital data while omitting:  
   - CDC 2014: 10-fold higher risk at age 1–9, 20-fold higher at ≥10  
   - WHO 2020: traditional circumcision in Africa has up to 35 % complication rate  
   - Meatal stenosis: 5–10 % of circumcised boys (Joudi 2011)

5. **Sexual-function evidence is presented one-sidedly**  
   The claim of “no adverse effect” relies on Morris & Krieger 2013 (disclosed circumcision advocate) while ignoring:  
   - Bronselaer 2013 (Belgian survey, n=1 369): reduced orgasm intensity, increased difficulty  
   - Sorrells 2007: foreskin is the most sensitive part of the penis to fine touch  
   - Frisch 2011: higher rate of sexual difficulties in circumcised Danish men

## Requested changes (author's proposals)

1. Move “Ethical and Legal Debates” higher in the article and give it more space  
2. Add a subsection on the anatomy and function of the foreskin, citing Taylor 1996, Cold & Taylor 1999 and Sorrells 2007  
3. Replace AAP 2012 expired policy with current AAP 2022 statement (neutral) and CPS/RACP/Dutch KNMG positions  
4. Include German court ruling, Brussels Collaboration, and UN CRC Article 24(3) interpretations  
5. Present absolute risk reductions and NNT for all claimed benefits  
6. State that non-therapeutic infant male circumcision is permanent surgery done without the child's own consent

In the author's view, these changes would bring the article closer to neutral, evidence-based reporting.
