# Author's-view review of the Circumcision article (2025-11-30, one-off)

This is an early, one-off review of the 2025-11-30 snapshot of the Grokipedia Circumcision article ([snapshot](../../snapshots/2025-11-30.txt)). It records the author's view, scored with a values framework the author was using at the time. It is not a finding of this repo's scan method: it has no published method or script, every score is a judgment, and it is not comparable with the sophistry scans described in the [README](../../../../README.md).

The original version used framework-specific terms and a simulated panel of named "judges". This file replaces `psi_square_evaluation.md` and restates it in plain terms; the original text is in the repo history at that path (for example in commit `eeb413e`).

## What the review said (author's view)

- Most of the article argues for non-therapeutic infant circumcision. The review reused the same day's one-off estimate of 68% pro from [bias_quantification.md](bias_quantification.md), which also has no published method.
- The article gives benefit statistics more weight than the permanent removal of the foreskin.
- The article treats parental proxy consent for non-therapeutic genital surgery as ethically valid without making clear that this is contested.
- Overall, the review judged that the article takes one side of a values question while presenting itself as neutral description.

The original also gave numeric scores on axes defined only within that framework (for example 32/100 on "polarity"). They are left out here because they have no meaning outside that framework.
